## E. Design system gaps

The renderer needs a small set of **deliberate image scales**: true bleed when a safe crop supports it, near-full-width landscape bands, and a generous but consistent inset. It needs gutter and minimum displayed-face-size rules so a group photo cannot pass geometry checks while becoming unreadable on a phone. A cover should have its own standard: clear subject, useful negative space only when intentional, and enough visual force at thumbnail size.

The current [ComposerEngine.swift](/Users/soumya/Developer/AK14/Sources/Core/Compose/ComposerEngine.swift) scores coverage, crop loss, hierarchy ratios, and arrangement repetition, but not whether the photos belong to one occasion or whether the rendered people are large enough. [Composer.swift](/Users/soumya/Developer/AK14/Sources/Core/Layout/Composer.swift) offers many placements around the optical center, which still produce the same centered-card impression across a carousel. The [style pack](/Users/soumya/Developer/AK14/Sources/Render/Resources/StylePacks/starter-editorial.json) offers film edges, paper and tape, but no typography system (`fontIDs` is empty). Add one restrained, optional cover line or location/date treatment, rules for color and exposure continuity, and permission for design-free slides. Typography should identify a story, not fill empty space; film edges should require a reason tied to the photos.

## F. Taste engine plan

The direction in the [taste design](/Users/soumya/Developer/AK14/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-26-taste-engine-design.md) is sound: deterministic safe generation, a model judging actual renders, human pairwise labels, and cautious preference memory are sensible without training. But the proposed judge ranks *layouts of a supplied direction*. It cannot rescue this mixed spine or recover a missed photograph. Add separate human and model comparisons for **event split, photo selection, sequence, cover, and layout**, in that order.

Ten consenting events are enough to start the harness, not enough to declare taste solved. Include deliberately mixed-event runs, candid-versus-polished choices, and several owners; report results per owner and occasion. Label “would post either/neither” alongside A/B preference, since a judge can reliably choose the less bad of two unpostable candidates. A 96-pixel-per-slide strip may conceal face size, awkward crops, and texture; evaluate that representation against larger previews. The plan says “pairwise” but requests a full ranking and combines two reversed rankings with Borda counts: test whether this actually agrees with human pairs better than direct A/B calls.

Preference memory can learn from exports and removals, but those signals are ambiguous: someone may export the least bad option or remove a photo because of privacy. Keep explicit dislikes and occasion preferences separate from style affinities. User references are valuable when translated into observable traits such as scale, color, spacing, and type. I would defer monthly trend refresh until selection and story coherence improve; trends will not fix an arcade cover on a hill trip.
